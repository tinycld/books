To order products or services from a vendor before the vendor bills you, create a purchase order. A purchase order lists what you order and shows a total. It does not go into your books: a purchase order has no journal entry, and no report of your accounts counts it.

## Create a purchase order

To create a purchase order, choose New in the sidebar (the round + button on a phone) and pick Purchase order, under Vendors. In a web browser, the Vendor field has focus when the form opens.

1. Choose the vendor. Books fills the mailing address and the email from the vendor, and moves to Email.
2. Check the order date and the Ship to address. A new order starts with your business name and address from Settings → Company.
3. Add account lines and product lines, as on a bill. A product with a vendor part number shows it under the product name.
4. Choose Save (⌘S) to stay on the form, Save & close (⌘↵) to open the saved order, or Save & new (⌘⇧↵) to start another one.

The first save gives the order the next number, such as 1001. An admin can change the next number in Settings, under Numbering, on the Purchase orders card.

## Open a purchase order

A saved purchase order opens read only. It shows its lines with what is ordered, received and still open, the Billed and Open totals, and who created it. On an open order, choose Edit (E) to change it, Copy (C) to start a new order from it, or Download PDF (⌘P). To delete an order, choose More, then Delete purchase order. A closed order cannot be edited.

For the line tables a new order shows, see [Settings](help://books:settings).
