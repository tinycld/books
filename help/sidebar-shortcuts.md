The Books sidebar has these parts, from the top: the Find in menu box, Shortcuts, the fixed screens of Books under a line, New and Reports. To find an item in any part, type part of its name in the Find in menu box; see [Moving around Books](help://books:navigating-books). On a phone, the sidebar is the Books menu: swipe in from the left edge of the screen to open it.

## Shortcuts

Shortcuts is your own list of the places you open most. To open one, press it. At first the list holds Customers, Vendors, Products and services, Profit and Loss, and Balance Sheet.

To change the list, press Customize shortcuts, the pencil beside the Shortcuts heading. In the dialog:

- To add a shortcut, press Add shortcut, type a few letters and pick from the list. You can add a Books screen, a form (for example New invoice), a Books report, also one that you saved, and the register of an account.
- To remove a shortcut, press the × on its row.
- To change the order, drag a row by its handle, or press the up and down arrows on the row.
- To get the first list back, press Reset to defaults.

Press Save to keep your changes, or Cancel to drop them. Nothing changes in the sidebar until you press Save.

The list holds at most 30 shortcuts. When it is full, remove one to add another.

The list is yours: each person has their own, and Books shows it on every device you sign in on. If you have read-only access, Add shortcut offers no forms.

A shortcut to a report or an account that was deleted, or that you can no longer open, is not shown in the sidebar. In the dialog its row reads Not available; remove it there.

## New

To start a transaction, open New in the sidebar and pick an action. A line separates the customer actions, the vendor actions and the other actions. The arrow beside the New heading folds the part and opens it again; Books remembers how you left it. On a phone, press the round + button: the Books menu opens with New open, at the top of the menu. If you have read-only access, New and the round + button are hidden.

## Reports

To open a Books report, open Reports in the sidebar and press the report. The reports that come with Books are listed first, in groups with a line between them: overview, sales, expenses and accounting. Then come the Books reports that you saved or that are shared with you. The report opens inside Books, so the sidebar stays beside it. A report that you open from a figure of another report, and the copy that Save as makes, also open inside Books. When you open a Books report from a link and press its back link, Books shows the list of the Books reports.

To search the reports, to make a new one or to share one, press All reports at the end of the part. It opens Reports with only Books reports shown. See [Reports](help://books:reports).

## The fixed screens

Under the Shortcuts, after a line, the sidebar holds every part of Books in a fixed order: the Dashboard, Banking, Sales, Expenses, Lists, Chart of Accounts, Journal Entries, Reconcile, Taxes and Settings. See [Moving around Books](help://books:navigating-books).
