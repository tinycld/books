To set up Books, open the setup step. In the organization setup it is the Accounting step. If Books was added later, choose Finish setting up Books at the top of the Lists page. Only a Books admin can run setup. On the Books setup page, other people see "A Books admin must finish setting up Books."

Setup has three parts. The label at the top shows which part you are on, for example "Part 2 of 3".

## Business

Enter the legal business name. It is required and can have up to 200 characters. Then choose the month your fiscal year starts in. The default is January. Books uses US dollars, and you cannot change the currency here.

## Ownership

Choose how the business is owned. This only decides your equity accounts.

- **One owner or partners** creates Owner's equity, Owner's contributions and Owner's draws.
- **Corporation** creates Common stock, Paid-in capital and Retained earnings.

To change an earlier answer, choose Back. Your answers stay as you typed them.

## Starter chart

This part lists the accounts that Books will create, grouped as Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Income and Expenses. To create them, choose Create followed by the number of accounts, for example Create 34 accounts. While it works, Books shows how many accounts it has made. You can change, make inactive or delete any account later in the [Chart of Accounts](help://books:chart-of-accounts).

If you want to build your own chart, choose Start with an empty chart. Books saves your business name and fiscal year and creates no accounts. You can then add accounts one by one or import a file.

## If the book already has accounts

When the book already has accounts and setup was not left half done, this part says that the book already has accounts and that Books will not add the starter chart. Choose Finish setup to save your business name and fiscal year.

## If an account is refused

If Books cannot create an account, it stops and names the account and the reason. If it could not save your business settings, it says so instead. Choose Try again. Books skips the accounts that already exist and continues. If you leave the page and come back after Books saved your business name, setup opens on the Starter chart part and picks up where it stopped. Start with an empty chart is not offered after a stop. Back is not offered once Books has created an account, so it keeps your earlier answers.

## When setup is done

Books shows how many accounts your book has. Choose Open Chart of Accounts to see them. When opening balances are available, you can also choose Enter opening balances.
