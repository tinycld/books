To see how your documents print, open Settings, choose Company and press Preview PDF. Books creates a sample invoice as a PDF file and downloads it. In a web browser, the browser saves the file. In the mobile app, the share sheet opens so that you can save or send the file.

The preview uses the saved company details. If you change a detail, press Save before you preview. Any person with access to Books can preview; only an admin can change the details.

## What a document shows

- Your logo and brand color. An organization owner or admin sets them in the app settings, under Branding. See [Organization branding](help://core:organization-branding).
- Your legal business name, address, phone, email and website from Settings, Company. If the business name is empty, the organization name prints.
- Page numbers at the bottom right of each page, such as "Page 1 of 2". On a long document, the column headings repeat at the top of each page.

Documents print on US Letter paper.

## Names in other scripts

Names in Latin (with accents), Greek, Cyrillic, Hebrew and Arabic print with the built-in fonts, and Arabic letters join correctly. In pointed Hebrew (Hebrew with vowel points), a dot can print in the wrong place, for example the dot of the letter shin. Names in other scripts, such as Chinese, Japanese, Korean or Devanagari, print only if the server has a font for them. Otherwise each character prints as a box. Ask the person who runs your server to add a font for the script.

To change company details, see [Settings](help://books:settings).
