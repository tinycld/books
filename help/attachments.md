To keep the vendor's bill or a receipt with the bill or expense it supports, attach the file to it. Everyone who can open the bill or expense can open its files.

The Attachments section is under Memo, on the bill or expense form and when you open a posted bill or expense. On a phone it comes after Memo.

## Attach a file

- In a web browser, drag the files onto the dashed area that says "Drag files here or choose them". When you drag over it, it says "Drop files to attach".
- Or choose **Attach files** and pick one or more files. On a phone, Attach files offers Take photo, Photo library and Files, so you can photograph a paper receipt.
- With a keyboard, press ⌥A. It works while the cursor is in a text box, such as Memo, and only while the Attach files button shows.

Each file can be up to 20 MB. Books keeps PDFs, images (JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, HEIC and HEIF), text and CSV files, and Excel and Word files. A bill or an expense holds at most 25 attachments. Books tells you under the dashed area when it does not take a file, for example "scan.zip is not a file type Books keeps. Attach a PDF, an image, or an office or text file."

## When a file is stored

- On a new bill or expense, a draft, an edit or a copy, a file that you add says "Not saved yet". It is stored when you choose Save draft, Post or Post & new. If you choose Cancel first, Books asks "Discard changes?", and Discard drops the files.
- On a posted bill or expense, a file uploads at once. Its row shows the progress, for example "Uploading… 45%".

If a file does not upload when you save, Books saves the bill or expense as a draft and tells you, for example "The bill was saved as a draft. 1 attachment did not upload. Save draft or Post tries it again." The file's row shows why, with Retry and Remove. Choose Save draft or Post again to try every file that did not upload, or Retry to try one file at once (on a draft that is saved).

## Open and download a file

To open a file, choose its row. Images, PDFs and text files show in the preview; for other files, choose Download. Previous file and Next file move through the files of the bill or expense. Download saves the file in a web browser, and opens the share sheet on a phone. Esc closes the preview.

A file that is not saved yet does not open.

## Remove a file

To remove a file, choose the × at the end of its row.

- On a posted bill or expense, Books asks first, for example "Remove receipt.jpg? It is deleted from this bill."
- On the form, the file goes from the list at once, and is deleted when you save. Cancel and Discard keep it.

You cannot add or remove files on a void bill or expense.

## What Edit, Copy, Void and Delete draft do

- **Edit** of a posted bill or expense: the form lists the original's files under "From Bill 1003" (or "From Expense 1003"). You can open them, but not remove them there. Files that you add are stored on the edit. When you post the edit, the original's files move to it, so the posted edit holds all of them, and the original (now void) says "Moved to 1003 (edited)".
- **Copy**: the new bill or expense starts with no files. Attach its own vendor's bill or receipt.
- **Void**: the files stay, and you can still open them, but nobody can add or remove one.
- **Delete draft**: the draft's files are deleted with it.

## Who can do what

With bookkeeper or admin access you can add and remove files on a draft and on a posted bill or expense. With read-only access you can see the list, open files and download them. While Books is offline or not taking changes, Attach files and Remove are not available; you can still open a file.

Files count toward your organization's storage.
