---
title: Moving around Books
summary: Use the sidebar, the New menu and the Lists hub.
---

To open a list, choose Lists in the sidebar and pick a card. To start a transaction, choose New (the round + button on a phone) and pick an action; the menu is hidden if you have read-only access. The Accounting group in the sidebar folds with its arrow. To change company details, the close date, numbering or access, choose Settings in the sidebar; see [Settings](help://books:settings). To work with accounts, see [Chart of Accounts](help://books:chart-of-accounts).
