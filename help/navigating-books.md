---
title: Moving around Books
summary: Use the sidebar, the New menu and the Lists hub.
---

To open a list, choose Lists in the sidebar and pick a card. On a phone, Books opens where you left it. To get back to Lists on a phone, choose All lists at the top of the Customers or Chart of Accounts list. You can also swipe in from the left edge of the screen to open the sidebar, then choose Lists. To start a transaction, choose New (the round + button on a phone) and pick an action. If you have read-only access, the menu is hidden. The Accounting group in the sidebar folds with its arrow. To change company details, the close date, numbering or access, choose Settings in the sidebar; see [Settings](help://books:settings). To work with accounts, see [Chart of Accounts](help://books:chart-of-accounts).

To enter the balances your accounts held on your starting date, see [Opening balances](help://books:opening-balances).

To write and review entries, which you open from the sidebar, see [Journal entries](help://books:journal-entries).

To run a report, choose Reports in the sidebar; see [Reports](help://books:reports).

To keep the people and companies you sell to, see [Customers](help://books:customers).

To keep the people and companies you buy from, see [Vendors](help://books:vendors).
