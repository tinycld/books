---
title: Vendor credits
summary: Record a credit a vendor gives you, with account lines and product lines, and post it.
tags: [vendor credits, purchases, vendors, refunds, returns]
order: 42
---

To record a credit that a vendor gives you, for a return, a refund or an overcharge, enter a vendor credit. Choose New, then Vendor credit. Pick the vendor, enter the vendor's credit no., add account lines and product lines for what the credit is for, and post it. When you post it, Books gives it a number and records it in your books: Accounts payable goes down by the total, and the lines' accounts are credited by the net of the lines (each line's account changes by its amount, a negative line the other way). The read view shows the credit's Unapplied credit, the part that is not applied to a bill yet.
