A vendor is a person or a company that you buy from. To see your vendors, choose Lists in the sidebar and pick the Vendors card. On a phone, Books opens where you left it. To get back to Lists on a phone, choose All lists at the top of the Vendors list. You can also swipe in from the left edge of the screen to open the sidebar, then choose Lists. A read-only user can open vendors but cannot change them.

## Find a vendor

To find a vendor in the list, type in the search field above the list. The list shows the vendors whose display name, company, email or phone contains the text.

To find a vendor from anywhere in the app, use the app search. Search finds vendors by display name, company or email. Choose a result to open that vendor.

## Create a vendor

To create a vendor, choose New vendor above the list, or choose New and pick Vendor. New is at the top of the sidebar; on a phone, New is the round + button. Type a display name, which is the only field you must fill in.

Display names must be unique among vendors, and case is ignored, so “Acme” and “ACME” are the same name. A vendor and a customer can have the same name.

To save the vendor and go back to the list, choose Save. To save it and start the next vendor at once, choose Save and new.

## Edit a vendor

To edit a vendor, choose its row in the list, or open the menu on its row and choose Edit. Change the fields and choose Save. Only the fields you changed are written.

If you try to leave a vendor that has changes that are not saved, Books asks "Discard changes?" first. Choose Keep editing to go back to the form, or Discard to leave without saving.

On a very wide window, the vendor opens in a panel beside the list, and the list stays in use. On a smaller window or a phone, the vendor opens as its own page over the list. If you make the window wider or narrower, the open vendor moves between the two and keeps what you typed.

## Payment terms and account number

Payment terms tell when you usually pay this vendor. Choose None, Due on receipt, Net 10, Net 15, Net 30 or Net 60.

Account number is your account number with this vendor, for example the number on their invoices.

## Default expense account

The Default expense account is the account that a purchase from this vendor usually goes to. The list offers your active accounts. It does not offer inactive accounts, Accounts receivable or Accounts payable.

If the account you chose becomes inactive later, the vendor keeps it until you change it.

## Make a vendor inactive

To hide a vendor that you no longer buy from, open the menu on its row and choose Make inactive. Inactive vendors do not show in the list. To see them in the list, turn on Show inactive. To use a vendor again, open the menu on its row and choose Make active.

## Delete a vendor

To delete a vendor, open the menu on its row and choose Delete, then choose Delete in the dialog. A deleted vendor cannot be restored, so the delete cannot be undone. If you delete the vendor that is open with changes that are not saved, the dialog tells you that those changes will be lost.

If you only want to stop using a vendor, make it inactive instead.

## Custom fields

An admin can add custom fields to a vendor form in Settings → Forms. Custom fields show before Notes on each vendor that uses the form they were added to. New vendors use the default form.
