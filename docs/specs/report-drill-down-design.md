Written in helix unattended mode on 2026-11-13 from the Books design spec §Scope ("One report shell (… **drill-down** …)") and §Ledger rules, core `docs/packages.md` ("Report sources", "Shipped and saved reports"), and the merged report designs that sent their navigation here (Profit and Loss Q7 and Cut, General Ledger Q6, Journal Q6, Balance Sheet Cut). **Revision 2, 2026-11-14:** the design gate accepted questions 1–10 as recommended and chose a hybrid direction: on a window of 1360 px or more the opened report shows in a side panel beside the statement (the Books list/detail pattern); below that it opens as a separate screen (direction A with the `from=` back link). The binding rule of 2026-11-01 applies: *design without the limits of what reports support today; what is missing is built in core first.* "(Qn)" marks a decision: Q1–Q11 answered at the first gate. **Approved 2026-11-14 (second gate):** Q12–Q16 accepted as recommended (the answers written here are final), and both platform-only items signed off: the web hover underline (item 1) and ⌘-click / middle-click opening the full report in a new tab (item 2).

## 1. Goal

A reader presses a figure on a Books statement and sees a transaction report that lists exactly the posted lines behind that figure (beside the statement on a very wide window, as its own screen otherwise), and presses a line on any Books line report to open the transaction that posted it; core's report engine and report view gain the general drill, open and drill-panel features that make this possible for every package.

## 2. Where it lives

- **Reports:** core's report view `/a/reports/<id>`. The opened report is a new Books shipped report, **Transaction Detail by Account** (Q2), listed in the hub `/a/reports?package=books` with the others. Books adds no screen and no route.
- **Where the opened report shows — the panel-or-screen choice lives in core.** The report route is core's (`app/a/(app)/reports/[id].tsx`, a `Slot` layout), so Books cannot wrap it the way its list layouts wrap records (`ListDetailLayout`). Core's `lib/drill.ts` `useOpenDrill` is the one press handler of every drilled figure (D1, D4): it reads core's `useDetailSurface()` (the window width against `DETAIL_PANEL_MIN_WINDOW` = 1360) and opens the **panel** on the source's own route when the surface is `panel` and the view hosts a panel, else **pushes the target report** with `from=`. So a drill from any core report view follows one rule on web, phone, native phone and native tablet (an iPad Pro 12.9" in landscape is 1366 px wide: panel; an 11" iPad in landscape is 1194 px: screen). The rule (threshold 1360, panel width 480) moves from Books into core (`@tinycld/core/lib/detail-surface`), and Books' list/detail layouts read it from there, so a Books record and a Books drill switch to a panel at the same width (Q15).
- **Records:** a line opens the screen that already shows its transaction: `books/invoices/<id>`, `books/sales-receipts/<id>`, `books/bills/<id>`, `books/transfers/<id>`, else `books/entries/<id>` (the rule of `lib/entry-route.ts` `entryHref`, Q4).
- **Server:** Books' Go `Register(app)` registers the new shipped report and the source change; the share sync (`server/report_shares.go`, `shipped_key ~ 'books.%'`) shares it with every Books reader with no change.
- **Core:** checkpoint 0 (§4) on branch `books/p5-drill-down` in `tinycld`, merged before the package PR.

## 3. Behavior

### Pressing a figure (D1)

- **Which figures open** (Q1, Q3, Q5):
  - **Profit and Loss**: Total of an account row (that account's lines in the period); Total of a section's heading row and of its "Total <section>" row (every account of that section's type in the period). % of income and the four computed rows (Gross profit, Net operating income, Net other income, Net income) do not open.
  - **Profit and Loss by Month**: each month cell of an account row or a section row (that month, cut to the period: a period that starts on March 10 opens March 10 – 31 from the March cell), and its Total cell (the whole period). % of income and computed rows do not open.
  - **Balance Sheet**: Current and Prior year of an account row (the account's posted lines from its first posted line up to the as-of day; for Prior year, up to the prior-year day its heading shows). Change, % change, class and section rows, and computed rows do not open.
  - **Trial Balance**: Debit or Credit of an account row (its lines from its first posted line up to the as-of day; for an income or expense account, from its first posted line in the as-of day's fiscal year). The retained-earnings row and the Total do not open.
  - General Ledger, Journal and Transaction Detail figures do not open: they are already the lines.
- A figure that opens is drawn like any other figure (`text-foreground`, tabular). On web, the pointer on it underlines it and shows a pointer cursor (spark 1). On native, a press dims it. A figure of 0.00 still opens, and the transaction report then lists the lines that cancel, or its empty state.
- **Press** (click, tap, or Enter on a focused figure) opens Transaction Detail by Account with:
  - **Period**: the source's own Period value when the figure covers the source's whole period (This fiscal year to date stays This fiscal year to date, All dates stays All dates, Custom stays Custom), else the range above as Custom ("March 10 – 31, 2026");
  - **Accounts**: the one account, or the section's whole account type ("All Expense accounts", the param's existing group pick);
  - every other option at its default.
- **Accessibility:** an opening figure is a link (`accessibilityRole="link"`) named "Show transactions: <row label>, <column heading>, <figure>" ("Show transactions: 6600 Rent or lease, Total, 14,400.00"), in the tab order on web. A group row's label keeps its fold toggle; its figures, which fold the group today, now open the drill instead, where the column has a drill.
- **No access:** when the reader cannot open Transaction Detail by Account (an admin unshared it), no figure is a link, no panel opens, and the report behaves as it does today.
- **In the builder** (Edit report): the preview draws drilled figures as plain figures; they do not open (the builder occupies the side of the screen).

### Where the opened report shows (D4, the hybrid)

- **Window of 1360 px or more, on the report screen: a side panel.** The press keeps the statement in place and opens the transaction report in a 480 px panel at the right of the report screen, under a left border; the statement narrows beside it (at 1440 px the statement keeps about 896 px). The panel's state is in the source report's URL (`d=<target report id>` and the target's own options under the `d.` prefix: `d.p-period`, `d.p-accounts`, `d.s`, …), so a reload keeps it; the source's copied link never holds it.
  - **The panel's header** (`report-drill-panel-header`): the target's title on one line ("Transaction Detail by Account", ellipsis when long); **Open as full report** (`report-drill-open-full`, an expand icon and the label) pushes the target report as its own screen with the panel's current Period, Accounts and sort and `from=<source id>`, whose back link then reads the source's title; **✕** (`report-drill-close`, "Close") closes the panel. Escape closes it too (a shortcut, standing approval).
  - **The panel's body:** the target's chips (Period and Accounts, which the reader can change; the change runs the panel's report only), its notices, its document header (business, title, period in words), and its table, fitted to the panel's width: Transaction Detail draws its phone layout there (each line wrapped: date · type · number; name · description; split · amount), with no sideways scroll. It sorts (the Sort chip of the wrapped layout), pages as the reader scrolls, and runs live. Its states are its own: loading, error with Retry, no access, empty with its action. Customize, Share, Save as, Copy link and Edit report are not in the panel: they are on the full report, one press away (Q13).
  - **One panel at a time.** Pressing another figure while the panel is open replaces its content (no second panel). Opening the source's Customize closes the panel, and a press on a figure closes Customize: one side surface at a time.
  - **The open figure stays marked** while the panel shows exactly its params (`bg-muted` on the cell, `accessibilityState.selected`); after the reader changes the panel's chips, no figure is marked.
  - **History** (Q12): opening, replacing and closing the panel are view changes on the source's route (`router.setParams`, as every other change of the report view), not history entries. Browser Back and Android Back leave the report as they do today; ✕ and Escape close the panel. (A history entry per open would remount the whole statement on every open and close, because the report route is a `Slot`.)
  - **Focus and screen readers:** the panel is a `complementary` region named by the target's title. On open, focus moves to the panel's title (web), so a keyboard reader continues in the panel; ✕ and Escape send focus back to the figure that opened it when that figure is still drawn.
  - **A line in the panel** opens its record (D2) like a line of the full report; the record screen's Back returns to the statement with the panel open (it is in the URL).
- **Window below 1360 px, and every phone: a separate screen** (direction A). The press pushes Transaction Detail by Account (`reports/<target>?p-…&from=<source id>`); the source report stays in history with its params, sort and Customize in its URL. Its header's back link names the source ("← Profit and Loss", D3).
- **The window crosses 1360 px with the panel open** (Q14): nothing remounts and nothing reruns (the Books pattern: only style values change). Narrower: the panel's view covers the statement as a page, and its header shows the back link "← Profit and Loss" in place of ✕, plus the title and Open as full report; the back link closes it. Wider again: it is a panel again.

### The opened report: Transaction Detail by Account (Q2)

- Shipped key `books.transaction_detail`, title "Transaction Detail by Account", hub description "Posted lines of chosen accounts for a period, grouped by account." Revision 1, **unpublished**, shared with every Books reader (the Trial Balance's model).
- **Header:** the Books business name, the title, the period in words.
- **Back** (D3, spark 2): on the separate screen opened by a drill or by Open as full report, the header's back link shows the source report's title after its back arrow ("← Profit and Loss") and returns to it as the reader left it (params, sort, Customize and an open panel, all in its URL). Opened from a copied link with no history, Back opens the source report with its defaults. Opened from the hub, it reads "Reports" as today.
- **Params (chips, in this order):** **Period** (`period`, `dateRange`, default This month to date; core presets, All dates, Custom) and **Accounts** (`accounts`, `choice`, multiple, from `books.accounts`, grouped by type in chart order with "All <type> accounts" picks: the Trial Balance's param). Both chips always show, so the reader sees what the drill chose. With no account chosen, the report lists the lines of every account.
- **Rows read:** posted lines (`posted = true`) of the chosen accounts dated in the period. Drafts never show; a void entry and its void reversal both show and cancel.
- **One section per account**, open when the report opens, labelled "<number> <full name>", in chart order (type rank, number, full name: the Trial Balance's keys). No line counts in headings (Q9).
- **Line rows** in date order, then posting order (`entry.posted_at`), then line position. Columns: **Date** (the label column, heading "Account / Date"), **Type** (the General Ledger's labels), **No.**, **Name** (the line's customer or vendor), **Description** (line memo, else entry memo), **Split** (the other account, or "-Split-"), **Amount**: the line by its account's normal side (debit-normal: debit − credit; credit-normal: credit − debit), so an expense reads positive and a refund to it negative (Q6).
- **Section total** "Total <number> <full name>": the sum of Amount. It equals the figure the reader pressed (pinned in Go and e2e); from a Trial Balance Debit or Credit it is that figure signed by the account's normal side (a 50.00 Credit on a debit-normal account reads −50.00).
- **Report Total** "Total": the sum of every shown Amount. In every drill the accounts are of one type, so it equals the pressed figure; with types mixed by hand it adds normal-side amounts, which the help topic says.
- **A large account pages** as the reader scrolls (core F7). **Sort** by Date, Type, No., Name, Description, Split or Amount inside each section (core F8). **Customize** (on the full report): Divide by 1000, Without cents, Negative numbers, red negatives; show or hide every column but Date.
- **Live:** any post, void, account edit, customer or vendor rename, or settings change runs it again, in the panel and on the screen.
- **States** (core): loading; error with Retry; timeout with Retry; no access; empty "No rows for <period>" with "Show this fiscal year", or "Clear filters" when Accounts is set.
- **Narrow widths** (`display.layouts` mobile: phones and the 480 px panel): each line wraps: date · type · number; then name and description; then split and amount. No sideways scroll. **Every width fits** (core column fit): Description, Split and Name narrow with an ellipsis first; Date, No. and Amount are never cut. The `oneLine` and tablet gates are measured for these columns in phase 3 pass 2 (lesson 30), at 390, 480 (the panel), 1024 and 1280.

### Pressing a line (D2, Q4)

- A **line row** opens the transaction that posted it, on the screen that shows that transaction: an invoice, sales receipt or bill line opens the document; a transfer line opens the transfer; any other entry opens the entry's read view. The rule is `entryHref` (`lib/entry-route.ts`), with the same rule in Go (§5) and a drift test.
- Where: the lines of Transaction Detail by Account (screen and panel), the General Ledger and the Journal, and the opened lines of the Profit and Loss and the Trial Balance. Beginning balance, group, subtotal, computed and total rows do not open (a Journal entry's heading row keeps its fold).
- The row's label is the link (`accessibilityRole="link"`, named "Open: <row label>"), and a press anywhere on the row opens it; on web the label underlines on hover. The push keeps the report in history; the record screen's Back returns to the report.
- What the reader may do on the record screen is that screen's rule.

### Help

`books/help/reports.md` gains "## See the transactions behind an amount": which figures open on each report, where the transactions show (beside the statement on a very wide window, else as their own report), the panel's Open as full report and Close, what the transaction report shows (the Period and Accounts chips the drill set, why its total equals the figure), Back, opening a line's transaction, and that Transaction Detail by Account also opens from the Reports list. Each "Read the …" section gains one sentence that points to it. Core `help/reports.md` gains D1–D4 (§4).

## 5. Data (Books)

### Source change (`books/server/report_sources.go`)

`books.journal_entries` gains `document` (Text): the id of the document an entry posts (empty for other entries). Pinned in `server/report_sources_test.go` (`CheckSources`). No migration: the column exists (`pb-migrations/2100000002_create_books_ledger.js`).

### One entry path rule for TS and Go (Q4)

`server/entry_routes.json` holds the document routes (`invoice` → `invoices`, `sales_receipt` → `sales-receipts`, `bill` → `bills`). `lib/entry-route.ts` reads it for `DOCUMENT_ROUTES`; the Go helper `entryOpenPath()` (`server/report_spec_helpers.go`) builds a `case` from it: a `document` entry with a document id and a listed doc_type → `concat("books/<route>/", [entry, document])`; a manual `transfer` → `concat("books/transfers/", [entry, id])`; else `concat("books/entries/", [entry, id])`. A Go test runs the formula over one line of each kind and compares with the JSON; a vitest checks `entryHref` against the same JSON. A new document type (expense, credit memo) adds one JSON row.

### The Transaction Detail by Account spec (`books/server/report_transaction_detail.go`, spec v4)

Built from the General Ledger's pieces (`server/report_general_ledger.go`, `report_spec_helpers.go`: the type-label `case`, `name`, `description`, `split`, the chart sort keys, `accountLabel`) and the Trial Balance's `accounts` param. `CN` = `creditNormalTypes()`.

- `source` `books.journal_lines`; `params`: `period` (`dateRange`, default `thisMonthToDate`), `accounts` (the Trial Balance's `optionsFrom` param: multiple, grouped by type, "All {group} accounts").
- `filter`: `and( eq([posted], true), between([date], {param period}), in([account], {param accounts}) )`.
- `columns`: `account` "Account / Date" (`[account]`); `type`; `number` "No." (`[entry, number]`); `name`; `description`; `split`; `amount` "Amount" = `if( in([account, type], CN), sub([credit], [debit]), sub([debit], [credit]) )`, `aggregate: sum`.
- `groups`: `[{ id: byAccount, col: account, open: true, subtotals: true, label: accountLabel }]`.
- `sort`: the chart keys (type rank, number, full name of `{record byAccount}`), then `[date]`, `[entry, posted_at]`, `[position]`.
- `detail`: `{ label: [date], open: entryOpenPath() }`.
- `display`: column fit as the General Ledger's for the shared columns (`type` `12ch`; `number`, `amount` `max-content`; `description` `flexGrow: 2`; `split` `20ch`; `name` `16ch`); `layouts`: mobile (date · type · number; name · description; split · amount), which also serves the 480 px panel, and tablet; `oneLine.minWidth` measured in phase 3 pass 2.

### Drills added to the existing specs (each report's `Revision` + 1)

`TD` = `books.transaction_detail`; `acct` = `{ record: byAccount, path: [id] }`; `firstLine(d)` = `lookup( source books.journal_lines, where and( eq([account], acct), eq([posted], true), lte([date], d) ), value [date], pick min )`; `firstLineFY(d)` = the same with `eq(fiscalYear([date]), fiscalYear(d))` added.

- **Profit and Loss** `amount`: `drill: { report: TD, levels: { byAccount: { period: { param: period }, accounts: acct }, section: { period: { param: period }, accounts: { group: { groupKey: section } } } } }`; opened lines: `detail.open: entryOpenPath()`.
- **Profit and Loss by Month** `amount`: the same `levels`, and `pivotCells: { period: { range: { from: { pivotBound: from }, to: { pivotBound: to } } } }`.
- **Balance Sheet** `current`: `levels: { byAccount: { period: { range: { from: firstLine({param asOf}), to: {param asOf} } }, accounts: acct } }`; `prior`: the same with the prior-year day (the column's `heading` formula) as `d` and `to`.
- **Trial Balance** `debit` and `credit`: `levels: { byAccount: { period: { range: { from: if( in({record byAccount [type]}, PL), firstLineFY({param asOf}), firstLine({param asOf}) ), to: {param asOf} } }, accounts: if( eq({record byAccount [detail_type]}, "retained_earnings"), null, acct ) } }`; opened lines: `detail.open`.
- **General Ledger**, **Journal**: `detail.open: entryOpenPath()`.

Why it holds: Transaction Detail's section total for account A over range R is the sum, over A's posted lines dated in R, of the normal-side amount. The P&L's account Total is the same sum over the same lines, so the drilled figure equals the total. For a section, the group pick "All <type> accounts" selects every account of that type, which is the P&L section's rows. For a month cell, R is the month cut to the period. For the Balance Sheet and Trial Balance, R starts at the account's first posted line on or before the as-of day (on the Trial Balance, its first in that fiscal year for income and expense accounts), so R holds every line the balance sums; on the Trial Balance the section total is the figure signed by the account's normal side. The panel and the screen run the same report with the same params, so the rule holds in both. Pinned in `server/report_transaction_detail_test.go`: `CheckSources`, `Load`, `Validate`; `Run` as a Books reader over a ledger built in the test (`newLedgerEnv`): chart order, normal-side amounts, Split, Name, type labels, drafts excluded, void pair, the `open` path of each entry kind against `entry_routes.json`; and for each drilled report, a run, the `@drill` params of chosen cells, then a Transaction Detail run with those params whose section total equals the cell (P&L account and section; by-month month cell and Total; Balance Sheet current and prior; Trial Balance debit and credit, a P&L account across a fiscal-year start; the retained-earnings row with no target).

## 8. Cut

- **Computed-row drills** (Gross profit, Net income, Retained earnings): a computed row adds and subtracts sections, so no one list of lines has it as its total (Q1).
- **Percent, Change and % change drills**: a ratio or a difference has no lines of its own (Q1).
- **Balance Sheet class and section totals**: its groups are not account types, so the Accounts param's type groups cannot select them (Q5).
- **Line counts in group headings** ("(14)"): dropped (Q9).
- **A bottom sheet on phones and a panel below 1360 px** (direction B's narrow half): below the threshold the opened report is its own screen (gate direction).
- **Customize, Share, Save as, Copy link and Edit report inside the panel**: they are on the full report, one press away (Q13).
- **A history entry per panel open** (Back closing the panel): the report route is a `Slot`, so each entry would remount the statement (Q12).
- **Editing drills in the builder**: the builder keeps them and shows them read-only (Q10); figures in its preview do not open.
- **Accounting method on the opened report**: Books is accrual only until p3-pay-bills.
- **Exports, print, email**: p5-exports.
- **The look of the screenshots** (green links, "Ask a question", the "Save As" button, uppercase totals): not carried.

## 9. Platform notes

- Hover underline on an opening figure or line label: web pointer only; native readers get the pressed dim, the selected mark of the open figure, and the help topic (platform item 1).
- Open in a new tab (⌘-click, middle click): web only; the link's `href` is always the full report's screen, so a modified click opens it in a new tab even where a plain click opens the panel; signed off at the design gate (platform item 2).
- Escape closes the panel: a keyboard shortcut (standing approval; web and hardware keyboards; ✕ on every platform).
- The panel itself is not platform-only: it follows the window width on web and native alike (a native tablet at 1360 px or more shows it; phones never do).
- Everything else (press, keyboard focus and Enter, Back, the new report) works on web and native.
