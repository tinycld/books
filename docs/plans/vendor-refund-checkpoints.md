## Decisions that apply to every checkpoint

- **Reference choices where the spec is silent (ledger, phase 3 pass 2, 1–8).** (1) The read view's list heading is "Vendor credits refunded"; it has no total row (the big figure is the total). (2) The read view's memo label is "Memo". (3) The cards' "Refund all" is a tick box with the label "Refund all" (the receive-payment "Pay all" pattern), not a Button. (4) A failed credits read says "Books could not load the vendor credits." (5) On the vendor credit, a credit with refunds and a balance shows the badge Partly applied (warning tone, `appliedBadge`'s); Refunded is the success tone. (6) "Not placed" is a strong line in the danger tone under "On vendor credits". (7) The no-credit state draws no totals and no "Clear refunds".
