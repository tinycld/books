## Plan decisions (where the plan settles what the spec or phase 3 left open)

- **D1 — percent places are the column's (checkpoint 0; human ruling 2026-11-17).** In core the places of a percent are on the formula node (`scale` of `percentOfTotal`, `percentOfParent`, `percentOfSection`), and the run result's column carries no places: the view and the exports print every percent with 1 place. Checkpoint 0 puts the places on the result column (`scale`, set only when the column's formula root is one of the three percent parts) and the view, the PDF and the Excel export print the column at it. **Default: 1 place** for every percent with no column `scale`: a `format: "percent"` column over another formula, a typed label, a param option. So a report whose percent column has no scale prints as today, and a percent-part column with `scale: 1` prints the same text as today (the Books Profit and Loss "% of income" and the stub's shipped percent column are `scale: 1`; the Books Balance Sheet "% change" is a `format: "percent"` over `if`/`mul`/`div`, so it has no column scale and keeps 1 place). One output does change, by the ruling: a percent-part column whose `scale` is not 1 now prints its own places. Core's builder gives a new percent part 2 places (`DEFAULT_PLACES`, `scaleFor`, `core/reports/lib/builder/formula-ops.ts` lines 34 and 493–501), so `tests/e2e/reports-period-pivot.spec.ts` lines 266 and 269, which pin a builder-made percent, change to "100.00%" and "60.00%"; lines 154–160 of that spec read the stub's shipped scale-1 column and stay. No other core or Books test pins a percent-part column whose scale is not 1 (plan review, 2026-11-17). The CSV is unchanged (it holds the plain number).
- **D2 — `percentOfTotal` on a two-level report (spec §4, the phase-4 pin).** Probed in phase 4 against core 87ed80f9 with core's own two-level inlined test report plus a `percentOfTotal` column (scale 2), Total 555: a plain customer 18.02; a family header 52.25; the parent's own row 36.04; the sub-customers 5.41 and 10.81; "Total <parent>" 52.25; the blank-key row 2.7; the Total 100; an opened row has no percent cell. So a customer inside a family gets its share of the report's Total and a family row the family's share: no core task. `TestSalesByCustomerShares` pins it on the Books spec.
- **D3 — the figures of an open family header.** Spec §3 Columns: "A family header row and its "Total <parent name>" row show the family's Sales and share". The engine's header row and its subtotal row both carry the cells (Go pins both). Core's view prints an open group's figures on its "Total <parent name>" row and a folded group's figures on its header, as on the Customer Balance and in the approved reference. Books does not change that; the e2e reads the family's figures on the total row while the family is open, and on the header after a fold.
- **D4 — the seed.** The seeded book already holds: a sale for a sub-customer (`seedCustomerBalance`: Harborview Kitchen, St. Jude gala), a posted sales receipt with no customer (`seedSalesReceipts`), a percent line on the Discount product, a subtotal line, a description line and the long-named product (`seedInvoices`, the posted invoice), a posted and a draft credit memo (`seedCreditMemos`). - "**What counts (Q2).** A sale line is an `item` or a `percent` line of a posted invoice, a posted sales receipt or a posted credit memo. Its figure is the line's stored amount, before sales tax, times the line's sign. A draft or a void document has no sale line that counts. `description` and `subtotal` lines never count (a subtotal line repeats the lines above it). The two reports read the same lines, so their Totals are equal for the same period and filters, and each Total equals the Subtotals of the posted invoices and sales receipts of the period less the Subtotals of its posted credit memos."
- "**Date (Q3).** A sale counts on its document's date; a credit memo lowers sales on the memo's date, whether or not it is applied or refunded. The reports have no Method choice: they are on the accrual basis."
- "**Sign (Q4).** Each sale line carries a sign: 1 for an invoice and a sales receipt, −1 for a credit memo (a credit memo's line amounts and quantities are stored positive). Every amount and quantity in both reports is multiplied by it, so a credit memo line shows below zero and lowers its customer's and its product's figures. A row can be below zero (a period with only a credit memo for that customer or product); it shows, with its negative figures. Refund receipts do not exist; the unit that adds one adds its lines to the same source with sign −1, with no spec change."
