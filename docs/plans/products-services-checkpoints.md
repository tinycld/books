- **The forms engine validates a save against the stored definition.** `useFormCreate().submit()` and `useFormEdit().patch()` rebuild `schemaRef` from the form row they loaded before they validate and split. The save path never uses it: a built-in hidden by type is still validated at save by the stored definition, every built-in's value still reaches `splitValues`, and the Income account required mark adds no save-time check. That is why `productColumnsFor` empties a bundle's own price, cost, accounts and purchase fields, and why the Income account required mark is display only (the server's "Choose an income account." is the check).
