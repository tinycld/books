## Plan decisions (where the plan departs from the spec's letter, and why)

- **D1 — the P&L one-line gate (phase 3 pass 2 spec gap).** Spec §5 `display` had no `oneLine` gate; without one core's fit puts the three columns on one line at 390 (labels cut to "4000 Service ..."), so §3's phone wrap never shows. Spec §5 now carries `oneLine: { minWidth: "600px" }` (edited in phase 4, the fixture's `PROFIT_AND_LOSS_ONE_LINE_MIN_WIDTH`), and checkpoint 4 ships it. The by-month report has no gate and no layouts (core automatic lines).
- **D2 — no Net income delta in e2e.** Spec §7 item 2 asked "Net income rises by 380.00 against a snapshot taken before". Other workers post today on the shared e2e DB, so a delta between two snapshots of a shared total is not stable. E2E asserts figures of rows it owns (its own accounts) and relations inside one snapshot (Gross profit = Total Income − Total Cost of goods sold, …); `TestProfitAndLossComputedRows` pins Net income over a ledger of its own. Spec §7 item 2 says so (edited in phase 4).
- **D3 — e2e count.** Spec §7 lists 10 items; with item 1 at desktop and phone and item 10 at 1280 and 1024 that is 12 runs, the limit. The plan uses 11: item 1's phone run folds into item 9 (the phone run opens the report from the Books menu at the phone width, the one phone-only path of item 1). Numbered in this plan 1–11 in checkpoint order: checkpoint 1 has spec items 1–5 (plan 1–5), checkpoint 2 spec items 6 and 8 (plan 6 Open an account, 7 Live), checkpoint 3 spec item 7 (plan 8 By month), checkpoint 4 spec items 9 and 10 (plan 9 Phone, 10–11 Fits at 1280 and 1024).
- **D4 — core-owned behavior.** Customize's panel, Expand all / Collapse all, the loading, error, timeout and no-access states, Copy link, Save as and the `pivotCap` notice text are core's view, driven by core e2e (`reports-view.spec.ts`, `reports-failure-states.spec.ts`, `reports-share.spec.ts`, `reports-group-pivot.spec.ts`) and by checkpoint 0's new spec. Books pins what Books owns: its specs (Go tests), its share (`server/report_shares.go`, unchanged), the statement's figures, and the fit of its two reports. Gate 2 compares `loading-*`, `error-*`, `timeout-*` and `no-access-*` nowhere: they are core's view over a static model, captured in phase 3 only.
- **D5 — widths are measured on the shipping route.** Phase 3 measured By month at 1280 inside the Books shell (with the Books sidebar): 11 months + Total + % wrap into core's automatic lines. The shipping route `/a/reports/<id>` has no package sidebar, so no checkpoint pins a by-month layout name or a width: checkpoint 3's gate 2 and checkpoint 4's e2e 10–11 read the layout core reports (`waitForTableFit`, which returns `'one-line'` or `'auto'` for this report) on `/a/reports/<id>` and assert the geometry of that layout (spec §3 accepts either). The P&L's `oneLine` 600px (D1) is measured again in checkpoint 4 on the shipping route (390 → `mobile`, 1024 and 1280 → `one-line`); a different result is reported, never patched by a new width.
- **D6 — month headings.** Until checkpoint 0 lands, the fixture's by-month columns carry the month words as plain column labels. Checkpoint 0's gate 2 (books side, after the merge) moves them to core's typed bucket label (P4) and re-captures the `month-*` states; the fixture and the re-captured shots are committed with checkpoint 1.
- **D7 — month words are pinned where they are written.** P4 writes the words in the client, so the Books Go test pins the typed month (`labelType` month) and the raw key ("2026-01"), not the text "Jan 2026"; the words are pinned by core's `format.ts` vitest (checkpoint 0) and by Books e2e 8 (checkpoint 3).
- **D8 — no-access runs.** The Trial Balance's precedent (`TestTrialBalanceNoAccess`): a user with Books level `none` runs the report without error and gets no account rows. For the P&L layout the five sections and four computed rows then read 0 (no posted amount reaches them).
- **D9 — inactive accounts.** The spec does not name the account state `inactive` (`books_accounts.inactive`), but an account made inactive after it posted in the period must still show, or Net income would not match the ledger. The filter has no `inactive` clause; `TestProfitAndLossPostedInPeriod` pins it.
- **D10 — P1 and the by-month spec.** P1's refusal "on a pivot's cell column" means: a pivot whose `cell` names a `percentOfSection` column is refused. A `percentOfSection` whose argument is the pivot's cell column (the by-month `pct` over `{col amount}`) is accepted and reads the row's total across the pivot, as P1's own text says. Checkpoint 0's tests pin both.
- **D11 — the by-month `amount` (B1, human 2026-11-11).** Core refuses a pivot `cell` column with no `aggregate` ("the pivot cell is a column with an aggregate"), so spec §5's by-month `amount` is `{ formula: signed, aggregate: "sum" }`, not `coalesce( sum(signed), 0 )`. Same figures: P2 fills a summed cell with 0 in a month with no line. The Profit and Loss (no pivot) keeps `coalesce( sum(signed), 0 )`.
- **D12 — English bucket words (human 2026-11-11).** P4's words are English for every reader, like the report view's day and range words and its en-US numbers; spec §4 P4 and core `docs/packages.md` say "in words (English)".
- **D13 — by-month topics (runner, checkpoint 3).** The Profit and Loss's fourth topic, `books_journal_entries`, comes from its detail label and its `[entry, sequence]` sort key; spec §5 gives the by-month report neither ("No `sort` keys on `[date]` / `[entry, sequence]`, no `detail`"), so it reads no entry field and its topics are lines, accounts and settings. Live re-runs still follow posts and voids: posting stamps each line, and a void posts reversal lines. The by-month Topics subtest pins the three topics and the absence of the fourth.
