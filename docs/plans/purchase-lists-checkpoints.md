## E2E rules

- Every `test.describe` title starts with `purchase-lists:`, as in `test.describe('purchase-lists: tabs and rows', () => { … })`. All new specs are `tests/e2e/purchase-lists-*.spec.ts`; shared steps go in `tests/e2e/purchase-lists-helpers.ts`.
- `login` / `navigateToPackage` from `@tinycld/core/e2e-helpers`; the area opens with a press on `books-nav-expenses` (`openPurchases`), always at the default viewport: a phone or 1440 run sets its viewport after that. No raw PocketBase writes, no `page.goto` after login.
- A seeded row is found by its test id (`purchases-row-<fixture id>`) after the test narrows the list to the row's vendor with the vendor picker or to its memo with the search field, never by position: the shared database holds many documents and the list loads 50 rows. Vendors are named by their full display names.
- A test that writes creates its own vendor (`createVendor`, `tests/e2e/vendor-form-helpers.ts`) and its own documents (`draftBillFor` and `postedBillFor` of `tests/e2e/purchase-lists-helpers.ts`, added in checkpoint 4; `vendorWithBills` and `openPayBills` of `tests/e2e/pay-bills-helpers.ts`), dated today, and finds them with the vendor picker.
- A refused write is forced for one record by its id (`refusePurchaseWrite`, checkpoint 4, on the model of `refuseInvoiceWrite` in `tests/e2e/sales-lists-actions.spec.ts`): a void from a list is a plain `PATCH` of the record and a delete a `DELETE`, not a batch request.
- A load error is forced with `page.route('**/api/collections/<name>/records**', r => r.fulfill({ status: 400, … }))` before the query's first load; the test waits for the failed response, and removes the route with `page.unrouteAll({ behavior: 'wait' })` before Retry. A loading state is held with `holdLoads` (`tests/e2e/record-form-helpers.ts`).
- Phone runs assert `purchases-cards` before the next action. A 1440 run asserts `purchases-names` after a document opens.
- A reader test has `(collaborator level)` in its title (the one-worker `books-access-level` Playwright project), signs in with `signInAsCollaborator` (`@tinycld/core/e2e-helpers`), sets the level at its start with `setCollaboratorLevel` (`tests/e2e/books-access-helpers.ts`), as `tests/e2e/sales-lists-reader.spec.ts` does, and restores the level in `afterEach`.
- No e2e posts a document dated before 2026-07-01 (`tests/e2e/books-settings.spec.ts` holds the close date 2026-06-30 for a few seconds).
- A test that creates a vendor and documents through the UI takes longer than 10 s; it creates the fewest documents its goal needs (the counts are in each test) and stays under the 30 s limit.

E2E budget for the screen: 12 tests (checkpoints 1 and 2: 0, checkpoint 3: 6, checkpoint 4: 6).
