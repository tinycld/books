## Plan decisions (where the plan settles what the spec or phase 3 left open)

- **D1 — the inline condition reads lines.** Spec §5: a family is inlined "when the group has no line whose customer has a parent". So a family whose only sub-customer nets to zero and is hidden under Rows = Non-zero still draws its header, the parent's row and its total. The fixture inlines by shown members; the two agree on every fixture row. Go pins the lines rule (`TestCustomerBalanceFamilies`).
- **D2 — the seed.** `seedCustomerBalance` posts four things, all dated in October 2026 and all with a memo that starts "Seed customer balance": the Harborview Kitchen invoice and the no-name Accounts receivable entry of spec §5, plus one invoice for "St. Jude Annual Spring Gala and Silent Auction Catering" with a long memo (a three-line name and a three-line transaction label on a phone) and one Accounts receivable entry that names the inactive "Old Mill Bakery" (a muted row in a dev book and in the gate-2 shots). Spec §5 says so (edited in phase 4). It runs last among the document seeds, so the earlier seeded documents keep their numbers. The seeded book already holds Accounts receivable lines with no customer (`PRIOR_YEAR_SEED` of `tinycld/books/lib/trial-balance-fixture.ts`, dated 2024-08-15, and a line of the seeded entries), so `noCustomerEntry` adds to an existing No customer row; no test pins that row's figure. The server refuses a new line that names an inactive customer (`checkLineParty`, `server/lines.go`), so the seed makes Old Mill Bakery active, posts, and makes it inactive again.
- **D3 — With activity in e2e.** The e2e reaches a zero balance by voiding the test's invoice (two clicks) and not by a payment in full (a second form, over the 10 s budget). It also proves "a void's reversal lines cancel the original". The paid-off customer and the overpayment are Go cases; checkpoint 2's e2e posts a part payment.
- **D4 — core-owned behavior.** Loading, error and Retry, refresh, Customize, Save as, copy link, share and the Export menu are core's view, driven by core e2e (`reports-view.spec.ts`, `reports-failure-states.spec.ts`, `reports-share.spec.ts`, `reports-export.spec.ts`). Books pins what Books owns: its spec (Go), its share, its figures, the rows of its CSV, the phone layout and the entry points. Gate 2 compares `loading-*` and `error-*` nowhere: they are core's view over a static model.
- **D5 — sort keys per level.** Core applies a formula sort key on every group level where it has a value, and skips a key that reads a deeper group's record (`levels.go` `readsRowValue`). Spec §3 says the customers inside a family are ordered "the same way" as the depth-0 rows, and also "the parent's own balance first"; §5 lists the balance key before the parent-first key. The plan decides, as the approved fixture does (`customer-balance-fixture.ts`, the by-balance state): the parent's own row is first under both orders. The order is therefore written as: parent's own row first (reads `byCustomer`, so level 2 only), then the balance key (every level), then No customer last, then the family name, then the customer name. Family rows read keys 2 to 4; customer rows read all five.
- **D6 — groups on hidden link columns.** `reports.Group.Col` names a spec column, so the spec has two hidden columns, `topKey` and `customerKey`, as the Sales Tax Liability groups on hidden `agencyKey` and `rateKey` (`server/report_sales_tax_liability.go`).

The Vendors list passes none. The reference has no shot of the Customers list; gate 2 judges the built header by itself.
